Opening Star Trail CleanR on Mac

Star Trail CleanR is free and not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS shows a
security warning the first time. This is normal and safe. To open it:

1. Drag Star Trail CleanR into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. macOS says it cannot be opened. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings (Apple menu, top-left).
4. Go to Privacy & Security and scroll down.
5. Find the line about Star Trail CleanR being blocked and click Open Anyway.
6. Enter your password or use Touch ID, then click Open Anyway again.

You only do this once. After that it opens normally.
